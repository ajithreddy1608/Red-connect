# red-connect-blooddonor

Create a clean, modern, and mobile-friendly website called BloodConnect — a platform that connects blood donors with nearby recipients.

The website should include:

A home page with a short tagline like “Connecting Donors. Saving Lives.” and a call-to-action button “Find Donors Near You.”

A “Find Donor” page where users can search by blood group and location.

A “Request Blood” form for patients to post urgent blood needs.

A “Become a Donor” page with a registration form (name, blood type, city, contact info).

A map or location section showing nearby donors or blood banks.

A contact page for support and emergency help.

A simple navbar with links: Home | Find Donor | Request Blood | Become a Donor | Contact

A footer with “© 2025 BloodConnect | Designed for Saving Lives.”

Style: Use a red and white theme, bold buttons, clean fonts, and friendly icons for blood, hearts, and maps.

Add small info sections like:

“Why Donate Blood?”

“How It Works” (Step 1: Register → Step 2: Get Matched → Step 3: Donate)

Make it professional yet compassionate — like a social impact website that encourages people to donate blood.
